Bloom is a modern, plant-centric web application designed to bring the joy of greenery into
every home—effortlessly. It combines e-commerce, subscription services, AI-powered guidance,
and community support in one cohesive user experience.
1- Mission & Value Proposition
●
●
●
●
●
Discover & Shop: Browse a curated catalog of indoor plants, succulents, and tropical
varieties, each with detailed sunlight/watering profiles.
Subscription Plans: Choose from Monthly Plant Boxes, Care Subscriptions, or Premium
Memberships for ongoing deliveries and exclusive perks.
AI Plant Assistant: Get real-time, personalized recommendations—everything from
which plant will thrive in low light to troubleshooting brown leaf tips.
Care Guides & Reminders: Receive step-by-step tutorials and automated
watering/fertilizing alerts so your plants never go thirsty.
Community & Support: Share feedback, read and leave reviews, or reach out directly
via support forms—all without leaving the site.
2- Key Features & Pages
I. Home (index.html): Hero banner, feature highlights, plan teasers, and best-selling plant
II. III. IV . V . VI. VII. VIII. IX. previews.
Plan Details (plan-details.html): In-depth descriptions of subscription tiers, side-by-side
comparisons, and sign-up CTAs.
Shop (shop.html): Full product grid with search, category filters, and pagination.
Product Detail (product.html): High-resolution imagery, care specs, user reviews, and
“Add to Cart.”
Cart & Checkout (cart.html, checkout.html): Manage line items, enter
shipping/payment info, and finalize orders.
AI Assistant & Care Guides: Interactive chatbot interface (embedded site-wide) plus a
dedicated “Care Guides” section with downloadable PDF tips.
Feedback & Contact (feedback.html, contact.html): Forms for user feedback and
general inquiries.
Profile & Orders (profile.html): Dashboard displaying recent orders, saved plants, and
account settings.
Authentication (login.html, register.html): Secure sign-in/up flows with client-side
validation and modal popups for TOS/Privacy.

HTML5 & CSS3: Semantic markup, CSS Variables for theming (leaf-green palette),

Why Bloom?
By uniting commerce, community, and care into one seamless interface, Bloom turns plant
parenting from guesswork into genuine delight. Whether you’re a first-time succulent owner or a
seasoned indoor gardener, Bloom helps your plants, and your home, thrive.
